Cultural authority

Community-origin knowledge is not treated as permission to reproduce. Living-tradition protocols require documented authorization from the relevant authority.

Removed

Unsafe or stale tools

Dose, interaction, legal-risk, RFRA-strength, live-law, and ceremony-protocol tools are not available in this edition.

Editorial rules

  • Primary research, reviews, statutes, opinions, history, and community-origin sources are classified separately.
  • No Nation, community, church, lineage, or tradition is represented as endorsing this atlas without explicit documented authority.
  • A cultural authority may request correction, contextualization, restriction, attribution change, or withdrawal.
  • Medical and legal outputs cannot return without qualified review, maintained provenance, versioning, and correction controls.

Correction or withdrawal request

Submission creates an editorial request; it does not concede ownership or determine authority.
