# Citation check, October 13, 2026

Checked by Claude for RN. Methods: every DOI was resolved in Crossref and its title, year, journal, volume and pages compared with the atlas entry; key findings for the trials at issue were compared with the PubMed abstract; legal citations were checked against the reporter or statute and the official source URL was fetched; trial registry numbers were checked against ClinicalTrials.gov. No source was invented. Statuses: **verified** (record found and the atlas entry matches), **corrected** (record found, the atlas entry was wrong and has been fixed), **not found** (no record found in the databases checked; the entry is kept and flagged).

## Totals

- Citation library (85 entries): verified 62, corrected 10, not found 13.
- Other cited items: neuroimaging table and page text corrected 5 (listed below); 1 citation added (Timmermann 2019 EEG, verified in PubMed).
